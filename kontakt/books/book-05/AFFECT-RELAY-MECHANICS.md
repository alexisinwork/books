# AFFECT RELAY MECHANICS — book 05

## Affect Relay
Care-service that uses a human carrier as part of embodied emotional regulation.

Client:
- keeps memories and narrative content;
- receives acute affect reduction;
- does not transfer identity or thoughts.

Carrier:
- receives induced affect-state parameters, not memories;
- really experiences distress;
- regulates it under medical supervision;
- contributes regulation-response data back into client care.

This is not magical conservation of emotion.

## Licensed carrier
Must have:
- chosen load classes;
- explicit per-session consent;
- cap;
- recovery protocol;
- medical monitoring;
- right to refuse;
- pay.

## Residual pool
Overflow capacity with structurally weak refusal:
- debt relief;
- housing credits;
- dependent care programs;
- precarious contracts.

Formal consent may exist. Practical freedom may be low.

## Client options
Possible legitimate options:
- reclaim/taper own affect;
- licensed relay;
- temporary acute relief;
- no relay.

The book must not imply one answer is morally mandatory for everyone.

## Recovery-contact
Supports orientation/body after load.
Does not normally receive load.

## Emergency split-relay
Rare medical procedure:
- second consenting adult receives part of autonomic load;
- non-certified use only when carrier faces immediate collapse and no licensed reserve is available;
- known rare risk: arrhythmia / autonomic injury.

## R5
Not anti-care.
Not anti-therapy.
Not maximal suffering.
Means Dall no longer treats his own emotional pain as somebody else's default job.

## Prohibited
- memories transferred;
- carrier literally becomes client;
- pain as moral purity;
- voluntary carrier automatically victim;
- Taras death framed as necessary price of victory;
- all Affect Relay shut down in finale;
- grief cured by one cathartic scene.
