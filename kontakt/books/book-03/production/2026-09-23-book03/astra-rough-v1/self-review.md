## Literary work still required in Terra

This rough is compact, especially several transition chapters. They are narrated scenes with actions and dialogue, not chapter briefs, but they are not a completed full-prose edition. Terra should develop lived duration, spatial detail, friction and voice across the whole32chapter text, including the latter half; it should not merely copy this rough and call the next phase complete. Do not inflate repeated definitions or permission speeches to obtain length.

The principal local weakness is the narrator's habit of explaining an already shown meaning, especially final lines and repeated negative assurances. Keep necessary evidence boundaries in the scene/state record, but make character interaction carry more of them. Courier, shop, stairs, frame, plane and food scenes should remain materially distinct. Lotz's correctness should not flatten his human impatience or uncertainty; Osya need not use Dal's analytic phrasing. The root's exact local grammar/physical/calendar notes are consolidated in `root-reading-notes.md` and must be handled in full Terra writing. They are neither individual author votes nor blockers requiring needless approval.
