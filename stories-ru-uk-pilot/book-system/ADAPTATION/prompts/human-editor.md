# Brief for an English-language fiction editor

Project: an English edition of a Ukrainian-language science-fiction novel, with a preserved Russian historical source for the first three books. The supplied English version has undergone model-assisted adaptation and checks. Assess the sample before quoting for the full book.

Requested work: fiction line editing for idiom, sentence rhythm, dialogue, register, character differentiation and emotional clarity, while preserving intentional ambiguity, dry humour and technical/bureaucratic voices. Use US house spelling. Supply tracked changes, a short style sheet and queries where an improvement could alter a clue, recurring image, plot fact or character intent.

Please distinguish line editing, copyediting and proofreading in your scope and quote. Beta reading is a separate reader-response service. A bilingual reviewer handles source fidelity; an English-only editor should flag uncertainty rather than invent facts. The final scope, fee, schedule and permission to receive files are agreed separately by the author. This brief is prepared but has not been sent to anyone.
