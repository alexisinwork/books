# Sol / Opus calibration and author style extraction

Preparation: select two coherent source passages, one rich in dialogue/humour and one in atmosphere/interior prose. Aim near 1200 words each without cutting a scene merely to hit the count. Freeze exact ranges, source hash, intent, glossary, voices, style and adapter contract. Both models receive identical inputs in separate sessions, without seeing the other's output.

The coordinator saves A and B under anonymous labels; model identities remain in a separate mapping unavailable to the evaluator until its comparison is frozen. Astra compares both against source and explicit intent, assessing fidelity, ambiguity, voice, comic function, rhythm and idiomatic target prose. Do not infer the model identity. Explain trade-offs using exact passages. The author chooses PRIMARY; do not manufacture a winner from numerical scores alone.

After the author revises one or two chapters, compare source / AI draft / author final. Extract candidate style rules with example IDs, before/after evidence, scope and exceptions. Distinguish local correction from global preference. Ask only about consequential uncertain generalizations. Record approved examples under narration, dialogue, humour, action and introspection; no minimum count guarantees quality.

Do not call this fine-tuning or assume persistent model learning. The next run must explicitly load the approved profile and examples. A preparation-only task creates ranges and forms, not translated samples.
