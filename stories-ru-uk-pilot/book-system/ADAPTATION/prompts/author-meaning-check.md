# Author-facing meaning check

For a disputed target passage, first produce an independent literal meaning paraphrase in the author's chosen working language using only the target passage and its immediate context. Then compare that recorded paraphrase with the canonical source and the explicit author-intent entry. Keep the two steps separate.

Present the original meaning, target reader's likely inference, any difference, whether ambiguity survives, and the smallest decision the author needs to make. Distinguish semantic confidence from literary effect. Back-translation may reproduce or conceal the same mistake; matching wording is supporting evidence, not proof of fidelity.

Preserve the exact target/source hashes and locations. Do not rewrite the source, resolve a mystery for the author, or approve the adaptation based only on this check.
